# Instrument A — Client Intake Form

Send one copy per clinic. Returned answers are ingested in Phase 5 with
provenance `client`. These fields effectively never exist on the open web.

---

```
CLINIC WEBSITE — INFORMATION REQUEST

Please reply inline. If anything doesn't apply, write "N/A".

1.  Exact clinic name as you want it displayed
2.  Doctor's full name + qualifications (degrees)
3.  Medical/Dental/Vet council registration number
4.  Years in practice
5.  Full street address incl. building, road, landmark, pincode
6.  Primary phone (with country code)
7.  WhatsApp number (if different)
8.  Email
9.  Exact opening hours, per day (note weekly offs)
10. Languages you consult in
11. Full list of services/procedures you offer  ← most important
12. Any fees you're comfortable publishing (optional)
13. One line: what makes your practice different from others nearby
14. Do you have an existing website? If yes, the URL
15. Your Google Business Profile link
16. Please attach: clinic logo (if any), a photo of the doctor,
    2–3 photos of the clinic interior
17. If you want patient reviews shown on the site, confirm you
    consent to displaying them (we will only use ones that comply
    with medical advertising rules)
```
